About

A self-hosted document scanner and archive system that converts physical scans into searchable PDFs using OCR, with tagging, full-text indexing, and metadata organization. Requires Docker/server deployment; no encryption at rest; demo available at demo.paperless-ngx.com (login demo/demo).
